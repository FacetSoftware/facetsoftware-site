01 — What it does
  • Find a patient by name, hospital number, specimen ID or episode number
  • Scan a specimen barcode instead of typing
  • Read results with high and low flags and reference ranges, and see what is still pending
  • Compare every result across all of a patient’s episodes in one timeline
  • Keep a ward-round list and see which of your patients have new results
  • See recent results for tests you requested, without searching
  • Open and share the official lab report PDF
  • Generate a ward-round handover sheet from a patient’s results
  • Reopen recently viewed patients straight from your device
  • Light and dark themes, for day rounds and night shifts
02 — On the ward

Built for the way a round actually runs.

One-handed, in a hurry, on hospital wi-fi. Results start appearing as soon as the first ones arrive rather than after everything has loaded, and recently viewed patients reopen straight from your device.

03 — Privacy

Your data stays on your phone.

Patient results travel between your device and the NHLS and nowhere else — we never see them, and we could not retrieve them if we wanted to.

Your NHLS credentials are held in your phone's secure storage and sent only to the NHLS. There are no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no crash-reporting SDKs in the app.

Results you have viewed are cached on your device so you can reopen them, and you can clear that cache at any time from the Recent tab or from Settings. Because it holds patient information, protect your phone with a passcode or biometrics, as you would any device you use to view patient records.

04 — Pricing

Free for five patients a day.

DeltaLab is free to use for five patients per day, resetting at midnight. Reopening a patient you have already opened that day does not count again, and patients saved on your device stay readable once the allowance is spent.

An optional subscription removes the daily limit. Monthly and annual plans are available, both with a free trial for first-time subscribers. Subscriptions are billed through Google Play or the App Store, and can be managed or cancelled there at any time.

05 — Important

DeltaLab is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by the National Health Laboratory Service. It requires your own valid NHLS portal account and an internet connection, and it shows only the records that account is already entitled to see.

It is a viewer for laboratory data and is not a diagnostic device — clinical decisions remain yours.
