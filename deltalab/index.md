# DeltaLab: NHLS lab results on your phone

> Stop chasing bloods. DeltaLab is a native iPhone and Android app for doctors in South Africa. Scan a specimen barcode or search by name, and it puts your patients' NHLS lab results on your phone: flagged against their reference ranges, side by side across episodes, and waiting on your round list. Sign in with the NHLS LabTrack (TrakCare) login you already have.

This is the plain-text version of <https://facetsoftware.co.za/deltalab/>.

## Scan. Tap. Sorted.

Point your phone at the barcode on a specimen tube. DeltaLab looks up that specimen, you tap the patient, and their results open with high and low values flagged.

## Know when a culture turns positive.

Add patients to your round list and tap Update. DeltaLab flags a new positive culture with its organism, and shows which tests are still pending for everyone else.

## See the change, not just the number.

Every result across all of a patient's episodes, in one table. High and low values are shaded, with arrows for the direction of change. Narrow it to one test set, or to abnormal values only.

## Your requests, already waiting.

The Yours tab lists recent results for tests you requested, over the last day, week or month, without a search. Add them all to your round at once.

## A handover sheet, filled in for you.

Generate a ward-round handover sheet from a patient's results: bloods by date with their flags, and cultures with their organisms, as a PDF you can share. The official lab report PDF is a tap away too.

## Built for the way a round actually runs.

One-handed, in a hurry, on whatever signal reaches the ward. DeltaLab is built to work over a weak mobile connection: results start appearing as soon as the first ones arrive, rather than after everything has loaded.

- Find a patient by name, hospital number, specimen ID or episode number
- Scan a specimen barcode instead of typing
- Reopen recently viewed patients straight from your device
- Light and dark themes, for day rounds and night shifts

## Your data stays on your phone.

Patient results travel between your device and the NHLS and nowhere else. We never see them, and we could not retrieve them if we wanted to.

Your NHLS credentials are held in your phone's secure storage and sent only to the NHLS. There are no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no crash-reporting SDKs in the app.

Results you have viewed are cached on your device so you can reopen them, and you can clear that cache at any time from the Recent tab or from Settings. Because it holds patient information, protect your phone with a passcode or biometrics, as you would any device you use to view patient records.

## Questions doctors ask.

### Do I need an NHLS LabTrack account?

Yes. DeltaLab signs in with your own NHLS LabTrack (TrakCare) login, and it needs an internet connection. There is no separate DeltaLab account to create, and the app shows only the records your NHLS access already permits.

### Is DeltaLab part of the NHLS?

No. DeltaLab is an independent app made by Facet Software. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by the National Health Laboratory Service.

### Where does patient data go?

Between your phone and the NHLS, and nowhere else. Results you have viewed are cached on your phone so you can reopen them, and you can clear that cache at any time.

### Is it a diagnostic device?

No. DeltaLab is a viewer for laboratory data. Clinical decisions remain yours.
